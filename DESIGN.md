# Design System: Temporary Placeholder

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Honest holding page"**

This records only the shipped temporary page. Its starkness communicates that the site is unfinished; it is not a permanent brand direction for the eventual introduction. No identity beyond the user's specified black-and-white placeholder has been chosen.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Single statement, no supporting UI.
- Full-viewport, flat ground.

## Colors

The page uses a pure black field and white text. Selection reverses them.

## Typography

The sole display line uses self-hosted Archivo Black; text reflows at narrow widths without shrinking into illegibility.

## Layout

A full-viewport grid centers the statement in both axes. The heading has a maximum width of 20 characters and the page has a 2rem inset. Narrow viewports wrap the words into two lines.

## Elevation & Depth

No shadows or dimensional treatments are used.

## Components

### Placeholder statement
A single semantic h1 inside main. There are no controls, navigation, or interactions on this temporary page.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** replace this provisional visual system when the actual site's purpose and positioning are defined.

### Don't:
- **Don't** treat this holding page as evidence of a decided permanent identity.
